Hermes — Privacy Policy Last updated: November 2, 2026 What this app is Hermes is a personal, self-hosted AI assistant operated by a single user on his own server. It is not a public service and has no other users. This page satisfies Google's requirement that an unverified app publish a privacy policy. What data is accessed With your consent, Hermes requests access to your Google account for these scopes only: Gmail — read, draft, and send email at your direction Calendar — read and write events at your direction Drive, Docs, Sheets — read and edit files at your direction Contacts (read-only) — to resolve names when you ask How data is used and stored All data is used only to perform the tasks you explicitly request on your own account. Access tokens are stored privately on the self-hosted server. Nothing is sent to third parties, advertisers, or data brokers. No data is sold, shared, rented, or transferred to any other party, and Hermes is not used to train any model. Data deletion Because this is your own account and your own server, you can revoke access at any time by removing the app from your Google account page (myaccount.google.com → Security → Third-party apps). Token files can be deleted from the server on request.
